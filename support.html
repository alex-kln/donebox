How does a block work?

Write what will be done when the block ends, pick a duration and start. When the time is up, finish or add 5, 10 or 15 minutes. Then mark the result: done, partly, or not done.

What is a flinch?

Every time you leave DoneBox during a running block, that counts as a flinch. Switching apps, going to the Home Screen and locking the phone all count as leaving.

Why did my block break?

In Strict mode, a block breaks if you are away for more than 10 seconds. The focused minutes before that still count. Choose Relaxed mode if you want the block to pause instead.

Why didn't I get the warning notification?

DoneBox sends one notification when you leave a running block, so you know you have 10 seconds to come back. Check that notifications for DoneBox are allowed in the iPhone Settings app.

Does DoneBox block other apps?

No. DoneBox blocks nothing and cannot see other apps. It only notices when you leave DoneBox, and shows you how often that happens.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.
