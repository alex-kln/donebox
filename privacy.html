DoneBox

Privacy policy

DoneBox collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What DoneBox stores

Your blocks (the outcome you write, duration, mode, result, next step, flinches and focus times), your settings, and the first name you enter during onboarding, if you enter one. All of it is stored only on your device, inside the app.

What DoneBox does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No servers. DoneBox never sends your data anywhere, to us or to anyone else.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • DoneBox does not see other apps or what you do in them. It only knows when you leave DoneBox.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, DoneBox schedules one local notification on your iPhone when you leave a running block. It is created on the device and is not sent through any server.

Backups

Like any app's data, your DoneBox data can be part of your device backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under your own settings, not by DoneBox.

Deleting your data

Swipe a block left in History to delete it. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

DoneBox collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
